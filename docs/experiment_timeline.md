# Scientific timeline

This is the dependency order of the research, not a claim that every older artifact has a reliable creation date.

1. Source and channel audits reduced DB9 to a physically defensible trusted-eight pose representation. Earlier narrower channel screens and anatomical interpretations were superseded or withdrawn where provenance was insufficient.
2. A frozen eight-class DB9 pose-only consumer was established independently of the later emg2pose substitution outcomes. Its DB9 internal posture utility does not prove cross-source calibration equality.
3. Single-user bridge and measured-versus-predicted pose diagnostics showed substantial substitution disagreement. Their endpoints and populations differ from the final cohort and are not final confirmation.
4. Gate4B's frozen multi-user cohort stopped before inference after source-authentic recording defects. It produced **no scientific outcome claim**. Gate4C used a separately frozen, structurally qualified successor cohort.
5. Gate4C characterized substitution heterogeneity on eight validation users within a 27-user, 926-file study. Median hard-output preservation was 0.6571 and median balanced preservation 0.3331; no binary pass threshold was preregistered.
6. Gate4D used 19 development users to study continuous Fisher-space error. Gate4E and Gate4F used development data to select and freeze separate ordinary and balanced Flip gates.
7. A prespecified joint confirmation then evaluated the frozen gates on 20 users untouched by CommonPose scientific development, using 702 paired files. Both primary paired user-risk reductions were positive in all 20 users. Upstream checkpoint training-user exposure remains unresolved.
