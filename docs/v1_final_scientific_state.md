# CommonPose V1 final scientific state

**Verified final result.** On 20 users untouched by CommonPose scientific development and 702 paired files, the frozen Gate4E ordinary Flip contract achieved a median paired 17.08 percentage-point risk reduction from full coverage to 30% within-user acceptance; the frozen Gate4F balanced Flip contract achieved 16.11 points. All 20 users improved on each prespecified primary contract, and both user-level one-sided sign tests survived Holm adjustment (p 1.9073486e-6 each).

**Absolute and secondary outcomes.** At 30% acceptance, E's median ordinary risk was 14.19%, while its balanced secondary risk rose to 68.40%. F's median balanced risk was 47.48%, while its ordinary secondary risk rose to 34.88%. These outcomes describe two distinct customer requirements; neither gate is a universal winner. Selective pooled ACC applies only to accepted blocks.

**Development evidence.** Gate4C showed heterogeneous output preservation across eight validation users. Gate4D's continuous Fisher-space result, and E/F candidate selection, were development-stage findings. The final lane confirmed the two frozen primary Flip contracts; it did not independently confirm every development mechanism or a global deployment threshold.

**Unknown or unestablished.** Upstream checkpoint training-user exposure, cross-source numerical physical calibration, population representativeness, human semantic gesture truth, online latency, future-user threshold coverage, and universal physical-state interoperability remain unresolved. The final cohort is CommonPose-development untouched, **not proven upstream-unseen**. This is an offline, bounded, consumer-specific selective-reliability result without a clinical or product safety claim.
