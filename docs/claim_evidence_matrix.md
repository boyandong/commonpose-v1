# Claim–evidence matrix

| Claim | Evidence | Status and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Predicted-pose substitution is uniformly reliable | Gate4C validation median hard preservation 0.6571, range 0.1632–0.7592 | Contradicted on observed users; no binary threshold was preregistered |
| Substitution risk varies across users | Gate4C validation distribution | Supported descriptively |
| A deployment-visible signal ranks continuous task-space error | Gate4D development median Fisher error 2.012 to 1.624 at 30%; 18/19 improved | Supported on development users for this distinct endpoint; no final continuous-error confirmation |
| Gate4E reduces Requirement-U ordinary Flip at 30% | Final paired median improvement 17.08 pp; 20/20 positive; Holm p 1.9073486e-6 | Supported on frozen cohort and within-user acceptance rule |
| Gate4F reduces Requirement-B balanced Flip at 30% | Final paired median improvement 16.11 pp; 20/20 positive; Holm p 1.9073486e-6 | Supported on frozen cohort; 47.48% residual median balanced risk |
| One gate improves every reliability objective | E balanced and F ordinary secondary median risks rose at 30% | Contradicted for the tested gate/endpoint combinations |
| Final cohort was untouched by CommonPose scientific development | Frozen cohort audit and final plan | Supported under available CommonPose provenance |
| Final cohort was unseen in upstream checkpoint training | Checkpoint-specific training-user membership unavailable | Unknown; cannot be inferred from CommonPose development status |
| Flip measures semantic gesture accuracy | No registered human-label truth in final substitution test | Not identifiable; measured-pose consumer output is the reference |
| 30% selective ACC is full-system ACC | Accepted blocks only; about 70% abstained per user | Contradicted |
| Universal interface or clinical safety is established | One frozen upstream/consumer path, unresolved calibration and deployment properties | Not established |

The numerical source is identified in [result provenance](../results/README.md). Statuses refer to the frozen V1 evidence, not to a future population or deployment.
